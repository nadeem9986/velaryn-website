# Velaryn Website PRD

## 1. Product Overview

Velaryn is an emerging technology startup based in Bengaluru, India. The website at `velaryn.in` will present Velaryn as a serious technology company building intelligent software for emergency response, healthcare coordination, public safety, and mission-critical operations.

The website must position Velaryn as the parent company, not as a single-product startup. Rescue Call may be mentioned subtly as an in-development initiative, but the primary message should remain focused on Velaryn's broader vision.

## 2. Product Goals

- Establish Velaryn as a credible technology company.
- Communicate the company's mission, vision, values, and focus areas.
- Support future conversations with hospitals, public agencies, partners, employees, and investors.
- Present Rescue Call carefully as an in-development emergency response initiative.
- Provide a clear contact path through `contact@velaryn.in`.
- Avoid unverified claims, customer logos, performance metrics, or launch promises.

## 3. Target Audiences

- Healthcare organizations and hospitals.
- Emergency response and public safety stakeholders.
- Government and smart city decision makers.
- Potential technology partners.
- Early investors or advisors.
- Future engineering and operations team members.
- Curious users learning what Velaryn is building.

## 4. Positioning

Velaryn should be positioned as:

- Early-stage, but serious.
- Technology-first, but human-centered.
- Focused on mission-critical systems.
- Based in Bengaluru, with global ambition.
- Building carefully before public launch.

Avoid language that implies Velaryn is already a large registered corporation, already serving customers, or already operating deployed products unless those facts become true later.

## 5. Core Message

Primary headline direction:

> Technology that powers faster, smarter emergency response.

Supporting message:

> Velaryn is building intelligent software for emergency response, healthcare coordination, and mission-critical operations, helping organizations make faster decisions when every second matters.

## 6. Website Scope

The first version should be a single long homepage with section-based navigation.

Required sections:

- Sticky navigation
- Hero section
- Trust/focus areas strip
- About Velaryn
- Mission and vision
- What we build
- Products and initiatives
- Technology focus
- Industries served
- Why Velaryn
- Roadmap
- Founders
- Careers
- FAQ
- Contact
- Footer

## 7. Content Requirements

### Hero

The hero should immediately communicate Velaryn's purpose and seriousness.

Required content:

- Company name: Velaryn
- Headline focused on emergency and mission-critical technology
- Short subheading explaining emergency response, healthcare coordination, and intelligent software
- Primary CTA: Explore Velaryn
- Secondary CTA: Contact Us
- Visual: abstract network, globe, map, or real-time systems illustration

### About

Velaryn is an Indian technology startup focused on building intelligent digital infrastructure for emergency services and healthcare coordination. The content should emphasize real-time communication, automation, and data-driven decision making.

### Mission

To build reliable technology that enables emergency responders, healthcare providers, and public institutions to coordinate more effectively and deliver faster, better outcomes.

### Vision

To become a global technology company developing innovative platforms that improve public safety, healthcare accessibility, and mission-critical operations through intelligent software.

### Products and Initiatives

Keep products high-level.

- Rescue Call: an in-development emergency response initiative focused on ambulance coordination and communication.
- Future Healthcare Platform: next-generation digital healthcare solutions.
- AI Solutions: research and development for enterprise and operational AI systems.

Use status labels such as:

- In Development
- Coming Soon
- Research & Development

### Founders

Mention names only for now:

- Yasar Ameen H N
- Nadeem

Avoid bios, photos, and external links until they are ready.

### Contact

Use:

- `contact@velaryn.in`
- Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

Do not add social media links until official accounts exist.

## 8. Design Requirements

The visual style should be premium, understated, and trustworthy.

Preferred direction:

- Dark premium theme
- Glassmorphism cards
- Minimal bento layout
- Aurora gradient accents
- Smooth Apple-inspired animation
- Clean typography
- Subtle motion, not distracting visual effects

Suggested typography:

- Headings: Sora or Space Grotesk
- Body: Inter
- Numbers: IBM Plex Sans

Suggested colors:

- Primary background: `#0A0A0A`
- Secondary background: `#111827`
- Card background: `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`
- Accent: `#3B82F6`
- Secondary accent: `#06B6D4`
- Success: `#10B981`
- Danger: `#EF4444`
- Border: `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`

## 9. Functional Requirements

- Single long responsive homepage.
- Smooth section navigation.
- Sticky navigation with blur on scroll.
- Contact CTA linking to email or contact section.
- FAQ accordion or expandable content.
- Mobile-friendly layout.
- Accessibility-friendly color contrast and keyboard navigation.
- SEO-ready metadata for Velaryn and velaryn.in.

## 10. Non-Functional Requirements

- Fast initial page load.
- Optimized visual assets.
- Responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- No layout overlap on small screens.
- No unsupported claims or misleading metrics.
- Easy to update later as company details become official.

## 11. Content To Avoid For Now

- Detailed internal product architecture.
- Machine learning model details.
- Unfinished product screenshots.
- Customer logos or testimonials.
- Unverified performance claims.
- Legal claims about company registration until registration is complete.
- Social links before official accounts exist.

## 12. Success Criteria

The website is successful if a first-time visitor understands:

- What Velaryn is.
- Why Velaryn exists.
- What areas Velaryn is building in.
- That Rescue Call is part of a broader company vision.
- How to contact the team.
- That Velaryn is early-stage but serious, trustworthy, and ambitious.
