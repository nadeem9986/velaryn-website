About Velaryn

Building digital infrastructure for intelligent products.

Velaryn is an Indian technology company focused on building intelligent digital systems that scale across emergency response, healthcare coordination, and mission-critical operations.

We develop robust software foundations around real-time telemetry, workflow automation, cloud infrastructure, and data-driven user experiences — enabling hospitals and emergency teams to work as one connected network.

Headquartered in Bengaluru, India

Engineered in India's leading technology hub, Velaryn combines rigorous operational standards with global ambition for healthcare and public safety technology.

Mission

Build reliable technology that enables emergency responders and healthcare providers to act faster.

To create thoughtful software platforms that simplify complex operational workflows, reduce response delays, and support critical decisions when seconds matter.

Vision

Become a technology company known for intelligent, mission-critical platforms.

To develop a portfolio of innovative platforms that combine resilient real-time engineering, practical AI automation, and accessible interfaces.

What We Build

Software foundations for mission-critical platforms.

Velaryn's technical direction spans operational software, healthcare technology, intelligent automation, cloud systems, and AI assistance.
